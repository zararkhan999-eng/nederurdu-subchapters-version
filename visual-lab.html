Pim Visual Lab

The art system behind NederUrdu: Pim's model sheet, the cast, the prop library and every word picture, drawn live from art/*.js. npm run art:build renders them to the SVG files the app ships.

Style rules

Flat, no outlinesShapes are solid fills. Ink (#1f2433) is only for faces, symbols and fine detail.
One hard shadeEach shape gets one darker copy offset down-left. No gradients.
Round everythingRound joins and caps; rounded corners on every box.
Pim acts it outVerbs and situations are performed by Pim or the cast; nouns stand alone, big and centred.
No answers in picturesDutch words never appear in art. Letters and numerals may, when they are the concept.
The same castAyesha, Imran, Sara and Ali, and neighbours Femke and Jan, recur across lessons.
