NederUrdu Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026 · App version 1.0.0

NederUrdu is an app for Urdu speakers who are learning Dutch. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Android app.

Summary

NederUrdu does not collect, send, or sell any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. The app does not use the internet.

Information stored on your phone

To remember where you are in the course, the app saves your learning progress (completed lessons, practice results, and your settings) on your own phone. This information never leaves the app through NederUrdu. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Phone backups

If you have turned on Android backup for your phone, Android may include the app's saved progress in your personal Google backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is controlled by you and Google, not by NederUrdu, and NederUrdu cannot read it.

Pronunciation audio

Dutch pronunciation is spoken by the text-to-speech voice that is already installed on your phone. If no Dutch voice is installed, the app can open your phone's settings so you can download one. NederUrdu does not record your voice and does not use the microphone.

Permissions

  • Vibration — for short haptic feedback on answers. You can turn this off in the app's settings.

NederUrdu does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

Children

NederUrdu is made for adults and older learners who are settling in the Netherlands. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
