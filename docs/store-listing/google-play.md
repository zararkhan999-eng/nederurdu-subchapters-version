`Learn practical Dutch for life in the Netherlands, explained in Urdu.`

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
NederUrdu helps Urdu speakers learn the Dutch they need for daily life in the Netherlands — explained in Urdu, step by step, from your very first word.

Start at zero. A0 begins with greetings, sounds, and the Dutch alphabet. A1 and A2 take you to real situations: introducing yourself and your family, the doctor and pharmacy, the gemeente and forms, school and childcare, work rosters, shopping and returns, transport, housing repairs, bills, and polite emails.

HOW EVERY LESSON WORKS
• Learn — each new word or sentence is taught first, with its Urdu meaning, pronunciation in Urdu letters, and regular and slow Dutch audio
• Understand — short grammar cards in Urdu (de/het, niet/geen, word order, past tense, omdat/dat/als)
• Practise — listening, choosing, building sentences with word tiles, and short typing
• Use — real situations, forms, and messages from Dutch life
• Check — a final check, with every mistake explained in Urdu and practised again

ALSO INSIDE
• 95 lessons across A0, A1, and A2, plus 28 real-life missions
• Review that brings back the words you find hardest
• Your personal word list with audio
• Dutch letters and sounds that do not exist in Urdu (ui, eu, uu, g, sch)
• Pim, a friendly cat on a Dutch bike, guides you

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Works completely offline — your progress stays on your phone

Pronunciation uses your phone's Dutch text-to-speech voice. If your phone does not have one yet, NederUrdu shows you where to download it.
```
