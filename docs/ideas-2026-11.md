# NederUrdu — new ideas (19 Nov 2026)

Ideation for graphics, learning experience, and "wow" features. Built on what already
exists: Pim, the code-drawn SVG kit (`art/*.js`), the living street, the journey town
map, passport stamps + tulip streak (`rewards.js`), skill-based spaced review and
missions (`app.js`), and recorded Piper audio. Effort: S = a day or two, M = about a week, L = more.

## Top 5 picks

| # | Idea | Why | Effort |
|---|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | **Gemeente letter reader** (real-document missions) | Your audience's #1 real pain is official mail; nobody teaches it in Urdu | M |
| 2 | **Truck-art × Delft blue visual theme** | A look no other app has; instantly says "made for us" | M |
| 3 | **Urdu ↔ Dutch contrast cards** ("Urdu mein aisa, Dutch mein aisa") | Targets the mistakes Urdu speakers actually make (V2 word order, de/het, g/ui/ij) | S–M |
| 4 | **Huisarts / school / gemeente roleplay** (branching, offline) | Practises a real conversation without needing AI or a backend | M |
| 5 | **Pronunciation mirror** (record & compare, no scoring) | First speaking practice that works offline and keeps audio on the phone | S–M |

## 1. Graphics & visual

1. **Truck-art × Delft blue theme.** Pakistani truck-art borders, peacocks and chamak
   patti panels drawn in the Delft-blue tile language. Use it as the frame for stamps,
   lesson-complete cards, and the unit headers. New `art/motifs.js` pieces in the existing
   kit; tokens in `brand-system.css`. **M**
2. **Two-city skyline map.** The journey map starts at a Lahore/Karachi-style bazaar
   (A0) and the road crosses to Amsterdam canal houses (A2), so progress *is* migration.
   Extends `map.js` scenes. **M–L**
3. **Seasonal living street.** `street.js` reacts to real dates: lanterns and a crescent
   for Ramadan/Eid, orange bunting for Koningsdag, sinterklaas boots, autumn rain, snow.
   Pure SVG layers driven by date. **S per season**
4. **Day/night + weather.** Sky colour follows the clock, so evening learners get a
   lamp-lit gracht. Small but it feels alive. `world.css` + `street.js`. **S**
5. **Calligraphic word reveal.** New Dutch words "write themselves" with an SVG stroke
   animation, then the Urdu meaning fades in nastaliq. Reuses `motion.js`. **S**
6. **Pim wardrobe.** Unlocks such as a shalwar-kameez scarf, an orange Koningsdag hat,
   a bike helmet, rain gear. Gives the tulip/stamp loop something to spend on. `pim-parts.js`. **M**
7. **Mouth-shape diagrams.** Side-view SVG mouths for g/ch, ui, eu, ij, uu that animate
   with the audio clip. Doubles as teaching (see LX #3). `art/kit.js`. **M**
8. **Dark mode "nachtmarkt".** A proper dark theme with warm lamp glows rather than
   just inverted colours. **S–M**

## 2. Learning experience

1. **Urdu–Dutch contrast cards.** Short side-by-side cards for the predictable
   transfer errors:
   - SOV → V2 ("main school jaata hoon" vs "ik ga naar school"; "kal **ga** ik")
   - no articles in Urdu → de/het with colour coding (de = blue, het = orange) used everywhere
   - sounds Urdu doesn't split: v/w, g/ch (close to Urdu خ / غ — use those letters as the anchor!), ui, eu, uu
   - Latin-script false friends for Roman-Urdu readers ("ja" ≠ jaa, "hij" ≠ "hai")
   New `contrast` question type in `course-data.js` + renderer in `lesson.js`. **S–M**
2. **Real-document reading track.** Realistic SVG mock-ups of a gemeente letter, a
   GGD appointment card, an energy bill, a school *ouderavond* note, a NS ticket. Tap
   any line → Urdu gloss; questions like "When must you respond?" Matches the A2
   roadmap's "recognisable forms" rule. **M**
3. **Listening-first warmups.** Each lesson opens with 20s of "just listen" (Pim says
   3 phrases, you tap the matching picture) before any text, which is good for learners
   who don't read Latin script yet. Uses existing audio. **S**
4. **Inburgering exam mode.** Practice sets that follow the real exam's format: KNM
   (Dutch society: huisarts, gemeente, rules), reading, listening, with a timer and
   a "would you pass?" meter. Builds on mission infrastructure. **M–L**
5. **Error notebook.** Every wrong answer is saved as a card ("you wrote X, it's Y,
   because…") and Pim brings them back in review. Shows mistakes as progress, not failure. **S**
6. **Dialogue shadowing.** Play a line, record yourself, play both. Slow clip →
   normal clip. Uses existing slow/regular audio. **S**
7. **Survival phrase pocket.** A non-lesson "sakhti mein" (emergency) card set:
   112, "ik heb pijn hier", "ik begrijp het niet", address — available offline from
   the home screen. **S**
8. **Mastery heatmap.** A per-skill grid (introduced → practiced → secure) so learners
   see what's strong and weak. The data already exists in progress. **S**

## 3. Wow features

### Quick wins
- **Shareable stamp cards.** A finished unit becomes a nice image (truck-art frame +
  stamp + Urdu caption) to send on WhatsApp. That's how this audience spreads things. Canvas from SVG. **S**
- **Haptics + sound on correct answers** (`navigator.vibrate`, Android wrapper). **S**
- **"Aaj ka lafz" home widget** (Android): a Dutch word of the day with an Urdu meaning. **M**

### Signature features
- **Gemeente letter reader.** A guided walkthrough of the 10 most common official
  letters (DigiD, toeslagen, belasting, gemeente, school). It's the feature people
  will tell their friends about. Content-only, offline. **M**
- **Branching roleplays.** Huisarts appointment, parent-teacher meeting, landlord call,
  supermarket. Pick replies from tiles; Pim reacts; different endings. Scripted, offline. **M**
- **Pronunciation mirror.** MediaRecorder, compare to the Piper clip, waveform overlay,
  and the mouth diagram. No scoring and no upload, so it's privacy-safe. **S–M**
- **Family mode.** Several local profiles on one phone (spouse, kids), each with their
  own Pim colour; a household "tulip garden" grows when anyone practises. No backend. **M**

### Moonshots
- **On-device speech check.** Whisper-tiny (WASM) or Android's on-device recogniser to
  confirm you said the right *word* (not accent grading). Big download, so make it opt-in. **L**
- **Camera "what does this say?"** Point at a Dutch sign or letter → on-device OCR +
  matched to course vocabulary with Urdu glosses. Needs an ML kit; Android wrapper only. **L**
- **AI tutor chat (Urdu-explained).** Optional, online, Claude-powered "ask Pim why"
  for grammar questions. Needs a backend/API key and a privacy policy update. **L**

---

## Suggested order
1. Contrast cards + error notebook + listening warmups (pure learning gain, small).
2. Truck-art × Delft theme + shareable stamp cards (visual identity + growth loop).
3. Gemeente letter reader + roleplays (signature content).
4. Pronunciation mirror + mouth diagrams (speaking, offline).
5. Exam mode, family mode, then moonshots.
